## Summary of Fixes Applied During Audit

1. **Storage Deletion IDOR Guard**: Added `${user.id}/` prefix verification in `useImageUpload.tsx` so users cannot delete other users' uploaded media.
2. **Crop Doctor Handler & Validation**: Added `handleImageUpload` with strict MIME enforcement (`jpeg`, `png`, `webp`), size check (`MAX_FILE_MB`), and image compression pipeline.
3. **MIME Whitelist for Avatar & Chat**: Blocked SVG and script uploads in `ProfileAvatarUploader.tsx` and `KisanChat.tsx`.
4. **Cart & Commerce Hardening**: Clamped cart item quantities to `>= 1`, ensured finite positive prices, and bounded coupon discounts in `AgriStore.tsx`.
5. **Wallet Ledger & Payment Security**: Eliminated negative debit amount injection, prevented wallet-to-wallet self-funding loops, and blocked duplicate refunds in `paymentStore.ts`.
6. **Dynamic Pexels Photography**: Integrated intelligent crop and product name normalization to dynamically query high-definition Pexels photography with zero rate-limit fallback.
